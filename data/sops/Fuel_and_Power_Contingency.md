# Fuel and Power Contingency Protocols (SOP v4.2)

## Section 4: Fuel Cargo Delay Response Plan

Coastal blizzards, sea ice pack formations, and coastal vessel delays frequently disrupt scheduled fuel deliveries.

### Section 4.1: Initial Disruption Ingestion
When a fuel cargo delivery (e.g., Cargo-Fuel-01) is flagged as DELAYED by 12 or more hours:
- The operational command platform must immediately calculate direct and transitive mission impacts.
- Dependent resupply missions (e.g., Operation Deep Freeze) must be marked as IMPACTED if planned start times precede estimated fuel arrival.

### Section 4.2: Rescheduling and Allocation Trade-offs
To resolve fuel deficits without compromising human survival:
- **Reschedule Mission Windows:** Shift dependent resupply missions by at least 24 to 36 hours to allow vessel unloading.
- **Depot Reallocation:** Reallocate up to 500 liters of diesel fuel from Depot Gamma or secondary staging yards to maintain base heating.
- **Priority Override:** Routine science deployments (e.g., Seismic Array Deployment) must yield transport assets to critical resupply missions.
